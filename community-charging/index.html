Why Community Charging

The right charger
for how your community gathers.

Level 2 chargers are built for overnight charging. DC fast chargers are built for the way people use community spaces — a few hours at a time. That makes your parking lot ideal for EV charging infrastructure.

Fast enough for a visit

A typical church service, community meeting, or event runs 1–3 hours. DC fast charging adds 60–200 miles of range in that window — a meaningful charge for most members.

Zero cost, zero risk

Enertech covers 100% of the equipment, installation, electrical work, permits, and ongoing operations. Your organization pays nothing — now or ever. If it doesn't work out, we handle removal.

Your organization earns

Every charging session generates revenue — a portion of which flows directly to your organization as a monthly revenue share. Passive income from your existing parking lot.

DC fast charging infrastructure carries significant cost. Funding is allocated through Colorado incentive programs and is limited — not every site qualifies. We'll assess your property and tell you honestly what's possible.

About Funding
Colorado's DC fast charging incentive programs — including Xcel Energy commercial EV rebates and state infrastructure grants — have limited funding pools. Enertech navigates these programs on your behalf and secures the funding that makes zero-cost installation possible. Qualification depends on your location, utility provider, site characteristics, and current program availability.
